# ChatGPT

Until the app is listed in the ChatGPT app directory, connect it as a custom app in developer mode (available on plans that allow developer mode; workspace admins may need to enable it).

1. Open **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Back in **Apps & Connectors**, choose **Create** (new app / connector).
3. Name: `Bill of Lading Data`. MCP server URL: `https://mcp.billofladingdata.com/mcp` (staging: `https://mcp-staging.billofladingdata.com/mcp`). Authentication: **OAuth**. Confirm you trust the app and choose **Create**.
4. The Bill of Lading Data page opens: paste your API key, check the spending settings, choose **Connect**.
5. In a chat, choose **+ → Developer mode** (or the app picker) and select Bill of Lading Data.

All 18 tools are annotated read-only, so ChatGPT treats them as reads. Paid calls above your limits and every contact or KYB unlock still ask for confirmation in the chat.
